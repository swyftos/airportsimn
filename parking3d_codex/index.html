How this illustration relates to the model

Seeded aircraft start from the supplied opening count. Illustrated hour demand = daily P50 − (day's peak cumulative net arrivals minus this hour's cumulative net arrivals) × mean aircraft space units from the supplied mix. Representative carry-in aircraft align the picture to that demand. Hourly placement is not a forecast; the probability beside it applies to the whole day. Waiting aircraft need a manager decision. The scene uses only recorded approvals on their applicable dates; the daily forecast can separately compare the full proposal. Arrival maneuvers reserve a stand; departing aircraft release their stand at the hour boundary. Carry-over adjustments stay at their illustrated positions and do not count as extra flights.
