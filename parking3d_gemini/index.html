Decision Governance & Data Provenance

What decision should your AI never be allowed to make?

“The model recommends; a named manager approves every action. It never accepts, refuses or reassigns an individual aircraft. Overflow is a flag for a person to resolve.”

Operational Rule: Aircraft are never refused automatically. When parking demand exceeds ramp capacity, extra aircraft stage in the highlighted "Awaiting Manager Decision Area" until a supervisor authorizes tow-packing, leases overflow apron, or coordinates PPR referrals.
