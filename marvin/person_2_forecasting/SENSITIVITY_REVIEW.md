## Historical backtest

The rolling 2025 test has 357 daily arrival evaluations. Its airport-pattern WAPE is 14.9% versus 16.6% for an eight-week weekday mean; its nominal 90% rolling range covered 87.9% of 273 evaluated days. Arrival and departure scores are equal because the input assigns an assumed 50/50 direction split. The scores test airport-level daily patterns
after a constant share conversion. They do not test FBO capture, aircraft mix, event demand,
or parking capacity.
