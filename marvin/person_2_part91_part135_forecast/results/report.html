Estimated from observed FAA counts

Daily history is observed FAA tower data at LAX (general aviation for Part 91, air taxi for Part 135). The 50% private-eligible share and 20% FBO capture are assumptions, so these are not observed FBO counts. Hourly curve, service times, prices and the VIP pause are synthetic placeholders.

Part 121 and military are excluded. Each part is forecast separately and summed; the 47/33/20 aircraft size mix is used throughout.
