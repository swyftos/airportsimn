Slot Advisor: Price Incentives & Reservation Analysis Economic Simulation

Evaluating demand-smoothing incentives: Drop-and-go credit vs cheap early arrival
Key Finding: Cheap Early Arrival Does NOT Free Parking

At 30% take-up, offering a cheap Thu–Fri slot moves unaccommodated aircraft from 2.3 to 4.3: aircraft arrive earlier but leave at the same post-game time, so they stay longer on the ramp and worsen congestion.

Past Super Bowls filled parking without raising prices; reservations (PPR) remain the primary control tool. Price incentives are optional offers, and the system never refuses an aircraft.
