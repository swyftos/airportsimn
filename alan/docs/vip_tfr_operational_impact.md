# Item 5: VIP/high-security TFR operational impact on private aviation

Aggregate/structural research only, per project guardrail §9 — no
specific individual is named or profiled. Public, government-sourced
TFR structure and documented enforcement outcomes only.

## Primary finding: the project's own event is itself a security-TFR precedent

Super Bowl LVI's own FAA Flight Advisory is issued under a **"99.7
Special Security Instruction"** TFR — the same regulatory category used
for presidential/VIP-level security events, not a routine stadium-event
restriction. This means the project already has a directly-applicable,
government-sourced, quantified example of exactly this question.

**Source**: https://www.faasafety.gov/files/notices/2022/Jan/SB_LVI_Flight_Advisory.pdf
(FAA Headquarters, Washington DC — primary government document)

**10NM inner ring** (center: SoFi Stadium, 335709N/1182038W):
- **All aircraft flight operations prohibited**, surface to FL180,
  **Feb 13 2022, 1430-2030 PST (6 continuous hours)**.
- Only exceptions: approved law enforcement, approved military directly
  supporting the event, approved air ambulance (all squawking assigned
  discrete codes); regularly scheduled Part 121/125 commercial carriers
  under TSA-approved security programs at Part 139 airports; Long Beach
  (KLGB) departures staying within Class D airspace.
- **This fully grounds Part 91 private GA traffic within the ring for
  the entire 6-hour window** — there is no general-aviation exception.
  LAX itself sits well inside this 10NM radius, so private GA at LAX is
  subject to the same prohibition as everywhere else inside the ring,
  with departures only possible via mandatory TSA Gateway Screening
  (24-hour advance registration required).

**10-30NM outer ring**, same 6-hour window:
- All operations prohibited **except** transit traffic on an active
  IFR/VFR flight plan with an ATC-assigned discrete squawk code and
  continuous two-way radio contact. No loitering permitted.
- Flight training, practice approaches, sightseeing, aerobatics, and
  several other GA activity types are explicitly banned throughout the
  entire TFR regardless of ring.

**Quantified takeaway for the model**: a VIP/high-security-level event
at this scale produces a **~6-hour full operational stop for private
GA within 10NM, and transit-only (no new local arrivals/departures
outside narrow exceptions) within 30NM** of the security perimeter.

## General VIP TFR structure (not Super Bowl-specific)

**Source**: https://www.nbaa.org/ops/airspace/alerts/notams/vip-tfrs/
(National Business Aviation Association — industry trade association,
not government, but the standard reference for operators)

- **Presidential-level**: inner ring typically 10-12NM, full GA
  prohibition below 18,000 ft — consistent with the SB LVI structure
  above, suggesting Super Bowl-week security is treated at a comparable
  level to a presidential visit.
- **Vice-presidential/other VIP**: smaller, typically 3NM. GA permitted
  inside if on an IFR flight plan and in ATC contact, but operators
  should expect **arrivals held and no ground movement** during the
  VIP's actual arrival/departure window — a short, specific disruption
  tied to the movement itself, not the whole TFR duration.
- **Ramp access**: presidential TFRs commonly include ramp closures —
  aircraft may be reachable via the FBO but not accessible on the ramp
  during the restriction.

## LA-region historical precedent (pre-dates this project's event)

**Source**: https://avweb.com/news/presidential-tfr-busted-six-times/
(trade press, not a primary FAA record — flagged accordingly)

- A past presidential visit to Los Angeles triggered TFRs covering
  **LAX, Santa Monica, Hawthorne Municipal, Van Nuys, and Burbank
  simultaneously** — the same set of airports relevant to this
  project's FBO-overflow discussion (Nazar's apron research, Elia's
  Super Bowl LVI daily OPSNET pull all reference these same fields).
- Documented enforcement: 6 pilots cited for TFR violations during one
  visit (FSDO investigation, possible certificate action); a separate
  visit saw 3 GA aircraft intercepted by F-16s for TFR violations. This
  confirms the restriction is actively enforced, not nominal.
- No precise hour-by-hour ground-stop duration found for this LA
  example specifically — the SB LVI advisory above is the stronger,
  quantified source for duration/geometry.

## Lower-confidence magnitude reference (not LA, included for scale only)

**Source**: trade press reporting on a 2026 Reagan National Airport
VIP security event — **flagged low-confidence**: single outlet, dates
inconsistent within the same article, FAA did not explain the
triggering event. Reported: ~2.5-hour ground stop, 303 flights
grounded, 118 cancellations. Included only as an order-of-magnitude
reference for how a VIP security event can ripple into commercial
schedule disruption at a major hub — not used as an LAX-specific or
GA-specific data point.

## Conclusion for the model

A VIP/high-security-level event at or near LAX should be modeled as
capable of producing a **full GA operational stop for several hours**
within a security perimeter, with the Super Bowl LVI TFR itself
(6 hours, 10NM full-stop / 10-30NM transit-only) as the best-quantified,
directly-applicable, government-sourced example. This is a structural
risk factor for the FBO's own event-week plan — a security-driven TFR
expansion (e.g., for an unannounced VIP attendee) during the planning
window could ground FBO traffic entirely for hours, independent of
parking capacity. This belongs in the guardrail/risk section of the
plan, not the demand forecast.
