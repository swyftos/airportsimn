## Based aircraft

Not listed on the page. No count, no breakdown by type, no annual
operations count. This agrees with the 5010 PDF, where fields 90-96
(SINGLE ENG, MULTI ENG, JET, HELICOPTERS, TOTAL, GLIDERS, MILITARY,
ULTRA-LIGHT) are all blank under "BASED AIRCRAFT — Source: Self-reported."

## Other facility notes (not independently verified beyond this page)

- Parking listed as "tiedowns."
- Fuel available; airframe and powerplant service both listed as "MAJOR."
- Multiple RAMP CTL frequencies, time-based by terminal taxiway.
- Taxiway D (between D7 and D8, north of Terminal One) closed to aircraft
  with wingspan over 157 ft; taxilanes C7/C9 closed to aircraft over 240 ft
  long. (Also present in the 5010 PDF remarks section.)

## Conclusion for the team

**LAX's based-aircraft count is not available from either public source
(FAA 5010 or AirNav).** Both are blank/silent on it. Do not log a based-
aircraft number for LAX as public data — treat it as a documented gap and
fill with a tagged assumption/benchmark estimate if the model needs one,
noting that LAX is an almost entirely commercial hub with minimal based GA.
