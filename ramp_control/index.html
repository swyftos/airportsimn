    How this illustration relates to the model

    Aircraft identities persist from the supplied opening fleet and modeled daily movements. Rounded size medians anchor the busiest illustrated hour; independent medians can differ from rounded daily movement totals. Any peak count discrepancy is shown in the overlay. Hour-of-day timing uses the supplied placeholder profile, not a forecast. Arrivals reserve stands and departures release them at the hour boundary; minute-level taxi paths are illustrative. Recorded named approvals activate preparations only on their applicable dates. No individual aircraft is dispatched or refused.
